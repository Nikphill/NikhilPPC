The 9 steps

StepWhat you doWhat you get
1 GoalsEnter budget, margin, LTV, deal value and close rates; split budget by platform.Break-even and target ROAS, target CAC and CPL, customers needed.
2 FunnelDesign the funnel by stage and platform; enter CPM, CTR and conversion rates.Forecast of leads, customers, CAC and ROAS per platform, with what it takes to hit target.
3 TrackingRecord pixel, tag and CRM IDs and tick off the tracking checklist.Progress score per platform.
4 EventsMap each business event to its Google, Meta and LinkedIn name.Gap flag per event.
5 DataLog weekly spend, clicks, conversions, customers and revenue per campaign.CTR, CPC, CAC, ROAS, status and a rule-based tip per row.
6 DashboardRead the roll-up by platform.Status vs target and budget-shift hints.
7 IncrementalityEnter lift, geo or on/off test results.True incremental conversions and an adjustment factor.
8 OptimizeFollow the weekly and monthly routine, look up fixes by symptom and log every change.A repeatable optimisation loop.
9 PacingEnter daily spend per platform for the month.Pacing vs budget, month-end forecast, daily spend needed and an On track / Reduce flag.

Good to know

  • Values on each page are sample numbers from a B2B example. Overwrite them with yours.
  • Ecommerce: set both CRM rates in Step 1 to 100 and read “leads” as purchases.
  • Platform-reported ROAS tends to over-credit itself, which is why Step 7 will estimate incrementality.
  • Rules of thumb (3:1 LTV:CAC, 20–30% profit buffer) are starting points. Confirm platform features in each platform’s own documentation.
  • Everything runs and is saved in your browser. No AI, no external services. Don’t enter passwords or customer personal data.
  • An earlier single-page version of the planner is kept at earlier-version.html until we decide which one to carry forward.
